    how it works

    Everything you do is drawn by hand, so it looks a bit shaky. That's on purpose.

    • Movement runs on your machine. Your browser simulates the walk and streams it to the database at 10 Hz, so it feels instant even on a bad connection.
    • The database is the referee. It re-runs the physics, rejects impossible input, and is the only thing allowed to score a kill. If the server disagrees with your frame, you snap back to where it says you are.
    • Kills make you fat. You take a share of your victim's mass. Heavy players move slower and hit slightly harder, but they're much easier to hit.
    • Bots fill the room. When people leave, the room tops itself back up with sketched bots.
